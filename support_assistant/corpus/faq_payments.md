# Zepto Payments & Discounts FAQ

## Payment methods

Q: What payment methods does Zepto accept?
A: Zepto accepts credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and cash on delivery in select areas.

Q: Is my payment secure on Zepto?
A: Yes. All payments are encrypted and processed through secure payment gateways. Zepto never stores full credit card details.

Q: Can I save multiple payment methods?
A: Yes. You can add and save multiple credit cards, debit cards, and UPI accounts in your Zepto wallet for faster checkout.

Q: Does Zepto accept international payment methods?
A: No. Zepto currently accepts only Indian payment methods and accounts.

## Wallets and prepaid balance

Q: Can I load money into a Zepto wallet?
A: Zepto doesn't have a traditional wallet system. However, you can save payment methods for quick checkout. Some promotional credit may be added to your account via cashback offers.

Q: How do I use promotional credit or cashback?
A: Promotional credit is automatically applied during checkout if eligible. It will be deducted from your order total before you pay.

Q: Can I view my promotional credit balance?
A: Yes. Check your account settings or the promotions section in the app to see available credits and their expiration dates.

## Discounts and offers

Q: How do I apply a coupon code?
A: Enter the coupon code in the "Promo Code" field during checkout. The discount will be applied if your order meets the minimum purchase requirement and the code is valid.

Q: Why is my coupon code not working?
A: Common reasons include: order value below the minimum, code expired, maximum redemptions reached, or code not applicable to your delivery zone. Check the code's terms before applying.

Q: Are there bulk discounts?
A: Yes. Orders above ₹1000 may be eligible for additional discounts. Check the "Deals" or "Offers" section in the app for current bulk discounts.

Q: Do student or senior citizen discounts exist?
A: Zepto occasionally runs special discounts for students and seniors. Check the "Offers" section or contact support for current eligibility.

## Billing and invoices

Q: Can I get an invoice for my order?
A: Yes. Invoices are generated automatically for all orders. You can download them from the app under "Order Details" → "Receipt."

Q: Is GST included in the displayed price?
A: Yes. All prices shown include applicable GST. Your invoice will itemize the GST amount.

Q: Can I pay with a business account or GST invoice?
A: Zepto B2B services are available in select areas. Contact business support for corporate account setup.

## Payment issues

Q: My payment failed but money was deducted. What should I do?
A: Don't worry. If payment fails, the amount will be automatically refunded to your account within 2-3 business days. If it's not refunded after this period, contact support.

Q: Can I use two payment methods for one order?
A: No. You must use a single payment method per order. However, you can apply a promo code and pay with a card in a single transaction.
