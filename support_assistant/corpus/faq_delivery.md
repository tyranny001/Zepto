# Zepto Delivery FAQ

## Delivery timeframe

Q: How quickly can Zepto deliver my order?
A: Zepto offers ultra-fast delivery in 10-30 minutes on most orders within our delivery zone. Exact timing depends on your location, order complexity, and current demand.

Q: What are Zepto's delivery hours?
A: Zepto operates from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM (10 PM on Sundays) in most areas. Exact hours may vary by city.

Q: Can I schedule delivery for a future time?
A: Currently, Zepto offers next-slot delivery only, typically within the next 30-60 minutes. Future scheduled deliveries are not available.

## Delivery charges

Q: Is there a delivery fee?
A: Delivery is free on orders above a minimum order value, typically ₹199-₹299 depending on your location. Below this threshold, a flat delivery charge of ₹30-₹50 applies.

Q: Do delivery charges apply on discounts?
A: No. The minimum order value is calculated on the actual order total after discounts and promotions are applied.

## Delivery coverage

Q: Does Zepto deliver to my area?
A: You can check delivery availability by entering your address in the app. Zepto currently operates in select cities and neighborhoods.

Q: What if my area is not in the delivery zone?
A: If your address is outside our delivery zone, the app will display "Delivery not available." You can subscribe to notifications to be alerted when we expand to your area.

## Tracking and updates

Q: Can I track my order?
A: Yes. You can track your order in real-time through the app. You'll receive SMS/push notifications at key stages: order confirmed, out for delivery, and delivered.

Q: What if my delivery is delayed?
A: If your delivery is delayed beyond the estimated window, contact our customer support team immediately through the app or call us. You may be eligible for a discount on your next order.

## Delivery issues

Q: What if my order doesn't arrive?
A: If your order doesn't arrive within 2 hours of the estimated delivery time, contact support immediately. We will either redeliver or issue a full refund.

Q: What if my order is damaged or incomplete?
A: Report the issue in the app within 24 hours of delivery. Include photos if possible. We will redeliver the missing/damaged items or provide a refund.
