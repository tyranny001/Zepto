# Zepto Returns & Refunds FAQ

## Return policy

Q: What is Zepto's return policy?
A: We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for most items if they are unopened and in original condition. Fresh items (milk, bread, fruits) are non-returnable unless damaged or expired.

Q: Are fresh groceries returnable?
A: Fresh items like milk, yogurt, bread, fruits, and vegetables are non-returnable under normal circumstances. We accept returns only if the item is damaged, expired, or defective at the time of delivery.

Q: How do I initiate a return?
A: Open the order in the app, tap "Return/Exchange," select the item, and choose the reason. Follow the in-app instructions to complete the return request.

## Refunds

Q: How long does it take to process a refund?
A: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and verify the returned item. You will receive a notification once the refund is credited to your original payment method.

Q: Can I get a refund for frozen items that thawed during transit?
A: Yes. Report this issue immediately upon delivery through the app. Provide photographic evidence if possible. We will issue a full refund or redeliver the item.

Q: What payment methods can be refunded?
A: Refunds are credited to the original payment method used for the purchase. If you paid via credit/debit card, the refund will appear in 5-7 business days.

Q: Is there a restocking fee for returns?
A: No. Zepto does not charge a restocking fee for valid returns of unopened, non-fresh items.

## Damaged and defective items

Q: What should I do if my order arrives damaged?
A: Report the damage in the app within 24 hours of delivery. Include photos of the damaged item and packaging. We will redeliver the item or provide a full refund.

Q: What if I receive the wrong item?
A: Contact support immediately. We will arrange a return pickup at no cost and deliver the correct item. If the correct item is unavailable, you'll receive a full refund.

Q: Are expired items eligible for return?
A: Yes. If you receive an expired item, report it immediately and we will provide a full refund or redeliver. You don't need to return the expired item.

## Exchange policy

Q: Does Zepto offer item exchanges?
A: Yes. If an item is damaged or defective, you can request an exchange instead of a refund. We'll redeliver the replacement item at no extra charge within 2 business days.
